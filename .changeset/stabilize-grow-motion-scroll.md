---
"@snapseq/stabilize": minor
---

- Motion: animations are no longer removed with `animation: none`. They run, and before each shot, and in `snapPage` after each grow once the DOM is quiet, they are finished, or cancelled when they cannot finish. A `forwards` reveal (a bar chart that grows in) keeps its final state, and a section that opens as the grown viewport shows it is measured open instead of being cut off or refused. Finishing runs the page's `animationend` handlers, and Playwright plays an infinite animation it cancelled for a shot again afterwards.
- New `scrollEvent` option, on by default: a scroll event at the document, bubbling to the window, after `snapPage` grows the viewport and before each shot, for UI that follows scroll events only (a tray that hides on scroll once its inline counterpart is in view, a back-to-top button). A "bottom reached" handler that appends content on each event can keep the page growing, and `snapPage` then refuses it; `scrollEvent: false` turns the event off. Needs snapseq's `afterGrow` hook, so the peer range is now `snapseq >=0.6.0`.
- New `floating` option, on by default: in `snapPage`, a `position: fixed` element keeps the top it had at the device viewport instead of following its `bottom` inset to the end of the grown page (a promo drawer, a chat bubble, a cookie banner). Fixed elements that were off the screen or only partly on it, that the page moved out of view or placed itself (a dropdown under its trigger), that the grow resized, or whose position the override does not reach (a transformed ancestor, a `margin: auto` dialog) are left alone, and the page's own inline `top` and `bottom` come back once the file is written. `floating: false` turns it off.
- A stylesheet that stays unreadable, such as a cross-origin `@import` of a font service's CSS, is fetched once per page load as text with CORS (at most 10 a shot, 512 K characters each, 3 s in all). The `cannot read <url>: its viewport units stay live` note is dropped only for a `text/css` answer with no viewport unit, escape or `@import` outside comments, strings and `url()`; `@font-face`-only CSS no longer puts the note on every shot.
